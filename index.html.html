A letter of love

Humnasheen

♡

For the one who feels like home

Tum ho, toh sab haseen hai.

Tum meri zindagi ka woh haseen hissa ho, jise main har dua mein phir se maangta hoon.

✦
🌙

In this life, I met many people, but I chose you as my Humnasheen.

👀

I chose you because you see me when the world overlooks me, you understand my silence, and you stand beside me not only in happiness but also in difficult days.

❤️

I chose you because you love me sincerely, without conditions or pretence. Life is given only once, and I want to live every moment of it with you.

🤍

I am blessed to have someone who truly loves me, and it is my promise to love you back with the same depth, honesty, and loyalty.

💞

I will love you beyond words… stand with you in pain, celebrate with you in joy, and become your peace when the world feels heavy.

🌧️

Because our love is not only about easy moments, it is about staying together when everything becomes hard.

🏡

You are not just my partner… you have become my home, my comfort, and my strength.

💖

I have found that kind of love in you, and I will hold it tightly, respect it, and protect it because not everyone is fortunate enough to experience something so real.

You are my home, my comfort, my strength.

Sirf tumhare liye

Meri Humnasheen

  • Tum meri zindagi ka sab se haseen shakhs ho ❤️
  • Tum ne mujhe sikhaya ke mohabbat asal mein kya hoti hai
  • Duniya ki bheer mein bhi tum ne mujhe dekha, meri khamoshi ko samjha
  • Har khushi aur har mushkil mein, main hamesha tumhare saath hoon
  • Tum mera ghar ho, mera sukoon ho, meri taaqat ho 🤍
